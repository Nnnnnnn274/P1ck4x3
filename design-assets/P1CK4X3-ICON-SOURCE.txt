P1CK4X3 uses an original green pickaxe mark.

The three 1024×1024 iOS app-icon variants live in:
lara/other/media.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/

- P1CK4X3-Light.png
- P1CK4X3-Dark.png
- P1CK4X3-Tinted.png

The mark intentionally has no small text beyond the P monogram, so it remains
legible on the Home Screen. Its dark-forest background, mint pickaxe, and
rounded border match the in-app P1CK4X3 interface.
